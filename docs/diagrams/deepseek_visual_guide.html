DeepSeek-v3-Lite

A from-scratch, raw-PyTorch reproduction of DeepSeek-V3 (~412M total / ~185M active parameters, 418.7M with depth-1 MTP head), featuring Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA), fine-grained DeepSeekMoE with aux-loss-free dynamic bias balancing, and μP learning-rate transfer.

Total Params: 411.6M (418.7M with MTP)
Active Params: 185.3M per token
Attention: MLA (d_c=512, d_R=64)
MoE: 1 Shared + 8 Routed (Top-2)
Supervision: Depth-1 MTP Head
